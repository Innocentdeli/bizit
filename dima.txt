Dima is not a passive SaaS dashboard or a course platform—it is an **Autonomous AI Growth Operator**. It replaces a fragmented $20,000/month growth agency stack (CMO, media buyers, copywriters, data analysts, and CRM managers) with an intelligent, self-optimizing cognitive engine.

## Step 1: Onboarding & Growth DNA Intake

When a company registers on Dima, it completes the **Growth DNA Wizard**:

1. **Brand Profile**: Enter company name, website, business model (SaaS, E-Commerce, Local Service, Marketplace, Agency, Creator, SME, Startup), and target audience.
2. **Historical Data Dropzone**: Upload existing CSVs, customer lists, ad spend history, or traffic metrics.
3. **Primary Growth Goal**: Select their immediate focus (*Customer Acquisition, Retention & LTV, Organic Virality, Conversion Optimization, or Marketplace Liquidity*).
4. **Autonomous Organism Diagnosis**: The Organism analyzes their data, calculates a **Health Score (0–100)**, identifies their primary growth bottleneck, and prescribes the exact starting growth engine with an instant preview campaign.

---

## Step 2: Domain Synchronization & Email Infrastructure

Before launching outbound acquisition or automated lifecycle loops:
- The company navigates to **Domain Sync** (`/portal/marketing/domain-sync`).
- Dima generates ready-to-use DNS records (**SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX**).
- Once DNS is connected, Dima verifies domain health, ensuring maximum email deliverability and protecting sender reputation for cold outreach and customer retention sequences.

---

## Step 3: Activating the 12 Autonomous Engines

Inside the **Growth Engine Studio** (`/portal/growth-engine`), companies have access to 12 specialized growth engines infused with top agency methodologies:

| Agency Superpower | Autonomous Engine in Dima | What It Does for the Business |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ogilvy & WPP** | Brand Architecture & Cognitive Resonance Engine | Generates core positioning, value propositions, high-converting copy angles, and brand messaging. |
| **VaynerMedia & BAMF** | 3×3×3 Creative Matrix & Content Velocity Engine | Creates 27 variations of content (3 hooks × 3 formats × 3 CTAs) ready for social syndication. |
| **Reforge** | Growth Loops & Retention Architecture | Constructs viral loops, referral mechanisms, and cohort retention sequences. |
| **KlientBoost** | Precision Ad Engine & Landing Page CRO | Designs multi-channel paid ad campaigns, headline variants, and landing page split tests. |

Companies can run individual engines or click **"Run All 12 Engines"** and export an executive-ready **Growth Blueprint (PDF)**.

---

## Step 4: Real-Time Market Intelligence & Gap Capture

Businesses don't need to manually research competitors:
- **Market Intel Hub** (`/portal/market-intel`): Monitors local and category supply vs. demand, saturation scores, and underserved customer searches.
- **Recommendations & Demand Surges** (`/portal/analytics`): Alerts the business when search demand for their services surges (e.g., *"+45% demand for emergency plumbing this week"*).
- **Executive Auto-Deploy**: The business clicks **"Capture Opportunity"**, and Dima's Autonomous Executive automatically provisions targeted ad campaigns and boosts ranking visibility.

---

## Step 5: Day-to-Day Inbound Operations & Autonomous CRM

When leads, inquiries, and reviews arrive:
- **CRM Inquiries** (`/portal/crm` → *Inquiries Tab*): Customer messages are captured in real-time. Dima's background AI sub-agents automatically draft and send tailored responses based on the business's profile.
- **AI Appointments** (`/portal/crm` → *Appointments Tab*): Customers book slots, and Dima automatically confirms bookings and dispatches calendar invitations.
- **Social Auto-Publishing** (`/portal/marketing/social`): Dima listens for positive 5-star customer reviews, extracts compelling testimonials, and automatically generates high-engagement social media posts.

---

## Step 6: Strategic Governance (AI Boardroom & Scenario Simulator)

For high-level executive decision-making:
- **AI Boardroom** (`/portal/ai-boardroom`): The company's founder or leadership inputs a strategic dilemma (e.g., *"Should we expand into B2B enterprise contracts or double down on self-serve SaaS?"*). Six specialized AI executives debate the decision:
  - 🧠 **Chief Strategy Officer** (Market positioning)
  - 💰 **Chief Financial Officer** (Unit economics & runway)
  - 📈 **Chief Revenue Officer** (Sales pipeline impact)
  - 🎯 **Chief Marketing Officer** (Customer acquisition cost)
  - ⚙️ **Chief Operations Officer** (Fulfillment feasibility)
  - 🩺 **AI Systems Doctor** (Risk & failure points)
  The Boardroom synthesizes all perspectives into a unified consensus action plan with downloadable minutes.
- **Digital Twin Scenario Simulator** (`/portal/growth-simulator`): Uses Monte Carlo simulation across variable sliders (*Ad Spend Multiplier, Pricing Shift %, Marketing Investment*) to forecast **Optimistic, Baseline, and Pessimistic 90-day revenue outcomes**.
